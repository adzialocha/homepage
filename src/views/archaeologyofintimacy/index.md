Releases September 15, 2025 on Warm Winters Ltd. as WW053

Recorded in Berlin 2023-2024 at Studio für Elektroakustische Musik (AdK) & in Marta’s home.

* Co-produced and mixed by Ludwig Wandinger
* Mastered by Adam Badí Donoval
* Fretless bass on "Flowers" & "Słowa (Not Saying a Word)" by Andreas Dzialocha
* Voice on "Spider Sister" and "Dreamers" by Rupert Enticknap
* Poem on "For the Night" written and read by Agnes Schneidenwind
* Voice on "For the Night" by Ella Olivia Bender
* Lyrics on "Dreamers" quoted from 'When Animals Dream: The Hidden World of Animal Consciousness' by David M. Peña-Guzmán
